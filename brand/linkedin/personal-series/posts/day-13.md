"I pressed jump! Why did I fall?" 🦘

Players press jump a split second after the ledge far more often than you think:

🔴 Strict ground check: off the ledge means no jump. The press is lost, the player falls, and the game feels unfair.
🟢 Coyote time + fast fall: allow a jump for a brief moment after leaving the ledge, and use heavier gravity on the way down so the arc feels snappy, not floaty.

Coyote time. Fall faster. Cut it short.
