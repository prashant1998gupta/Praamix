Some cameras make players feel sick. 🤢

Same player, same twitchy movement, two cameras:

🔴 Hard-locked follow: the camera copies the player exactly, so every small twitch shakes the whole screen.
🟢 Damped follow + look-ahead: the camera eases toward its target instead of snapping, leads in the direction you're moving, and ignores tiny movements near the centre.

Damp. Look ahead. Dead zone.
