Why do VR hands go straight through tables? 🖐️

Your real hand has no table to stop it. Your virtual one can.

🔴 Hand locked to the controller: wherever the controller goes, the hand goes, straight through the table.
🟢 Physics hand: a spring pulls the hand toward the controller, and the table stops it. When they split, a ghost shows where your real controller is.

Separate. Simulate. Show the gap.
