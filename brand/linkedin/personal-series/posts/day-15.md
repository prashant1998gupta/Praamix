Why does your character ice-skate? ⛸️

The character speeds up and slows down. Watch the feet:

🔴 Walk clip at a fixed rate: movement speed changes but the animation doesn't, so planted feet slide forward or back.
🟢 Speed ÷ stride length: measure how far one stride of the clip travels, then play the walk at move speed divided by stride. Feet stay planted. Blend into a run as speed rises.

Measure. Match. Blend.
