Teleport or smooth locomotion? The oldest fight in VR. 🥽

🔵 Smooth locomotion: push the stick and glide. Feels like walking, but for many players it's harder on the stomach.
🟠 Teleport: point, blink, arrive. Comfortable for most, but it breaks the flow.

Neither one wins for everyone. So:
Offer both. Add comfort options. Remember the choice.

Which should be the default in your game? 👇
