## 1. Create the page

Who does this: **you**, signed in to your own LinkedIn account. LinkedIn only lets a person with an account at least a few days old and with some connections create a page.

1. Go to **linkedin.com/company/setup/new** and pick **Company**.
2. Fill in the fields below, tick the box that you are authorised to act for Praamix, and press **Create page**.
3. On the new page, press **Edit page** and fill in the rest from section 2.

## 5. Keeping it alive (simple rhythm)

- **One post a week** is enough. Good subjects you already have: devlog entries, a new trailer or short clip from the YouTube channel, a before/after of an asset (the Portal Gun wireframe → finished on /services), a lesson from a Quest performance fix.
- **Short videos** (under 60 s, square or vertical, subtitled) do best on LinkedIn. The trailer and the logo reveal are ready-made.
- **Always label key art as key art**, and only describe features that are built.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Once the page is live, send me its address and I'll add LinkedIn to the website footer next to YouTube and Discord.
